# Provenance

This project is human-directed and AI-assisted. Final design authority,
acceptance criteria, and editorial control rest with the human author.
AI contributions were material and are categorized below by function.

## Human authorship

The author defined the project thesis, scope, schema design decisions, and
editorial framing. AI systems contributed research, case drafting, synthesis,
and implementation under author supervision; they did not independently
determine project goals or publication decisions. The author reviewed,
revised, or rejected AI-generated output throughout development.

## AI-assisted collaboration

### Thesis development, schema design, and editorial critique

Lead collaboration: ChatGPT (OpenAI). Key contributions to schema refinement
(renaming `dependency_type` to `control_layer`, adding `confidence`, tightening
field names), scope decisions (including Jackson MS as cross-level-of-government),
thesis refinement ("effective political control"), and sequencing guidance
(research before prototype, essay before map).

### Case research, implementation, and map construction

Lead collaboration: Claude (Anthropic) via Claude Code. Heavy contributions to
case research and sourcing across 15 cases, GeoJSON data assembly, MapLibre
map implementation, synthesis memo drafting, and taxonomy development.

## Provenance basis and limits

This document is a functional attribution record based on commit history,
co-author trailers, and the working conversation. It is not a complete
forensic account of all contributions.

Some AI contributions (especially design critique, rejected alternatives,
and sequencing decisions) may not appear in repository artifacts.

Model names/tools are recorded at the platform level (e.g., ChatGPT,
Claude Code); exact model versions may vary across sessions.

## What this document does not claim

- No exact proportional attribution. Contributions are categorized by
  function, not quantified by token count or lines produced.
- "Ideas that didn't ship" and "approaches rejected" are real contributions
  that leave no artifact. This document cannot fully account for them.
